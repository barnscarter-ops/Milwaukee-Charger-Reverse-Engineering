## New features (proposal and choice)

Candidates: (1) X-ray see-through of the other face, in register; (2) isolation zone map with barrier-crossing parts; (3) unpowered continuity log that builds neutral nets; (4) per-part photo evidence crops from both faces; (5) measurement ruler between two points; (6) guided tour of the power path; (7) printable part report; (8) side-by-side photo compare slider.

Chosen: 1, 2, 3. They use data only this project has (two registered photos, the traced keep-out, board positions), directly serve tracing and the isolation question in PROJECT_VISION, and work by tap on phones. Rejected: 5 (units are not measured, a ruler would imply false precision); 6 (would assert circuit functions not yet traced); 7 and 8 are routine. Candidate 4 was added as part of the record panel.

Constraints kept: zones and every derived label say `probable`; the continuity log states unpowered use and flags primary-to-secondary pairs; no values or functions are inferred.
